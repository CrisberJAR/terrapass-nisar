# TerraPass — 30-second video (English)

**How to produce it:** open `https://<your-app>.azurewebsites.net/promo.html` in Chrome or Edge on a computer with a GPU, wait for "Ready", then click **● Record video (30 s)**. The browser downloads `TerraPass-30s-film.mp4` (or `.webm`) at 1080p with a soundtrack. If you get a `.webm` file and need MP4, convert it with https://cloudconvert.com/webm-to-mp4 or with `ffmpeg -i TerraPass-30s-film.webm -c:v libx264 -crf 18 TerraPass.mp4`.
To add your voice: record the narration below on your phone and lay it over the video in CapCut or Clipchamp (built into Windows 11).
